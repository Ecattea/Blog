Housing debates are rarely about bricks alone. They are about **status**, **scarcity**, and who gets to keep a neighbourhood frozen in time.

![Apartments at dusk](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1560448204-e02f11c3d0e2?q=80&w=1200&h=800&auto=format&fit=crop)

## A simple test

If a city adds homes but still prices out the middle, the constraint is usually **permission**, not **materials**.
